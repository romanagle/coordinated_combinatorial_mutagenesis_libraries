### Problems solved
- Rejected the interpretation that `1st` contains 16S and `2nd` contains 23S values.
- Corrected the HIV projection after the batch selector favored repeated hits over the top-scoring hit.
- Fixed a `cmalign` bug: aligning the whole genome (instead of each hit's own window) let the repeated `mir-TAR` element (present at both LTRs) get stitched into one bogus alignment spanning nearly the full genome.
- Fixed ViennaRNA scoring rejections ("bad trusted structure") by normalizing multi-bracket WUSS reference structures to plain `()` notation before calling `easel compstruct`.
- Restored the deleted Human RNAmap per-hit, family-level, and binned-delta figures from intact batch results.

### Open problems
- Original `shape_16S.dat` and `shape_23S.dat` files are unavailable for exact reconstruction.
- The batch Rfam selector counts family occurrences instead of selecting the best-first significant hit.
- The HIV-only reactivity histogram is small (554/342 samples) and dominated by RRE alone, making it too noisy to trust as fold guidance.

### Questions
- Were pooled class values randomly divided before sorting into equal halves?

### Decisions
- Treat Eddy 2014 `1st` and `2nd` files as pooled splits, not RNA-specific datasets.
- Use the first significant `cmscan` row as the HIV RNA projection target.
- Benchmark all 11 inclusion-threshold HIV Rfam hits independently, not just the top RRE hit.
- Feed raw (unnormalized) SHAPE reactivity, not the normalized column, to the guided fold.
- Run SHAPE-guided folding against two chem-hist sources (independent RMDB corpus, circular HIV-only) and keep results in separate output trees rather than merging them.
- Replace the Human RNAmap-style scatter/family-scatter/binned-delta plot bundle with one per-hit bar chart ordered by ViennaRNA MFE, since N=11 made the original plots uninterpretable.
- Preserve the Human RNAmap plotting stage as a portable, documented workflow with example outputs.
