## Research insights

- FiLM clade omission identifies where the model uses phylogenetic context, but that can include background calibration and logit-prior shifts. Clade-specific identity-element claims need a second filter based on clade-vs-domain ISM/logo divergence.
- Frequency logos and IE logos should not be expected to agree directly. A base can be frequent in both groups while still having very different mutation sensitivity in the trained model.
- Broad clade hits should be decomposed hierarchically because the parent signal can be localized to a subset of child clades or opposed by other child clades.
