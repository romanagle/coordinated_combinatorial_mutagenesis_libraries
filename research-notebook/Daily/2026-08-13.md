- Use ViennaRNA's native Deigan model with default parameters `m=1.8`, `b=-0.6`.
- Do not wait for ShapeKnots completion before recording this session.
- Flatten ShapeKnots' predicted pseudoknot layer to unpaired before scoring with `easel compstruct`, matching the pseudoknot-blind representation already used by every reference structure and every other method in this benchmark.

- Interpret activity balancing as phenotype coverage, not mechanistic sequence-space coverage.
